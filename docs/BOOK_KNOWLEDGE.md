# Trading-book principles translated into BOom-Boom

This is an engineering interpretation, not a guarantee of profit.

- **Mark Douglas, Trading in the Zone:** execute a defined statistical edge consistently; accept uncertainty; never escalate risk after losses.
- **Alexander Elder, The New Trading for a Living:** test systems, define entry/stop/target before entry, control risk and keep a trade journal.
- **Steve Nison, Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques:** candle patterns are contextual tools, not a complete system; test convergence with trend/support/resistance/volume.
- **Van K. Tharp, Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom:** measure expectancy/R-multiples, include costs and size positions from risk.
- **Ernest P. Chan, Machine Trading:** use realistic backtests, out-of-sample validation and monitor strategy decay.

Required before live trading: chronological holdouts, walk-forward tests, no look-ahead bias, venue-specific fees/spread/slippage/funding, drawdown review, paper trading, exchange reconciliation and tested kill switch. These notes do not establish a profitable edge.
